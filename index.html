Equipment you need HarnessHelmetRock climbing shoes

Every session needs a harness, a helmet and rock climbing shoes. There are three ways to get them:

  • Borrow them from a friend (free)
  • Buy them from Crashpad or Decathlon (recommended)
  • Rent them from ClimbMT (members only)

If you're buying, I currently recommend the Beal NEO harness and the Simond Edge helmet from Decathlon.

Core sessions

Take them in order, or start at the one that matches your skills.

  1. 1

    Climbing Experience

    Duration: ½ day

    You need
    Nothing. Complete beginners welcome.
    You'll do
    A taste of real rock on top rope, with basic movement and safety. I handle all the rope work.
    Additional gear requiredNothing extra

  2. 2

    Learn to Belay and Climb Well

    Duration: ½ day

    You need
    Session 1, or the same experience from elsewhere.
    You'll learn
    Harness fitting, tying in, partner checks, top-rope belaying and lowering, plus footwork and movement technique.
    Additional gear requiredLocking carabinerATC-style belay deviceGRIGRI (optional)

    • ATC-style belay device. I recommend the Petzl Reverso.
    • Optional: a Petzl GRIGRI. I don't recommend it for beginners, but it's okay to use.

Optional sessions

Two extras for what happens at the top of a route.

Cleaning Sport Routes

Duration: ½ day

You need
Session 2, or the same skills from elsewhere.
You'll learn
Threading the anchor, stripping quickdraws, and getting down safely.
Additional gear requiredPersonal anchorExtra locking carabiner
  • A personal anchor: either a sling and locking carabiner, or a lanyard and locking carabiner. For a lanyard I recommend the Petzl Connect Adjust (the best) or the Simond lanyard. Any locking carabiner will do.
  • One more locking carabiner (any).

Abseiling

Duration: ½ day

You need
Nothing. No experience needed.
You'll learn
Setting up an abseil, using a prusik backup, and descending under control.
Additional gear required2 locking carabinersATC-style belay devicePrusik cord
  • Two locking carabiners (any).
  • An ATC-style belay device with two rope slots, such as the Petzl Reverso.
  • A prusik cord. Buy a sewn loop such as the Beal Jammy, or make one from about 1.5 m of 5 or 6 mm climbing accessory cord, tied into a loop with a double fisherman's knot.
